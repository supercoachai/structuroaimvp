Structuro is for ADHD brains that do not start

Structuro is a calm execution web app from Eindhoven, the Netherlands. It is not a planner and not an App Store listing. You pick today's energy, then one small first step. No streaks. No shame. If your bottleneck is seeing time, look at Tiimo or Structured. If it is a long list, look at Todoist. If it is the first click, that is Structuro.

Most ADHD productivity roundups cite iPhone apps. Structuro runs in the browser on phone and computer, and can live on your home screen. That is a real difference, not a missing feature to hide.

The product loop is short: day start, one focus block, optional shutdown. Energy first. Few tasks in view. Built with 50+ interviews and about a hundred ADHD testers. Not medical advice. Not a diagnosis.

Trial: 7 days, no credit card. Then €12.99 per month or €119 per year. Cancel in two clicks. Payments include iDEAL. Data stays in the EU.
