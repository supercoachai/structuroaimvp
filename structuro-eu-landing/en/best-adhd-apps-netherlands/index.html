How to choose an ADHD productivity app (Netherlands, 2026)

By Niels van den Hurk, founder of Structuro, who has ADHD himself.

The best ADHD task app in the Netherlands is not a ranking. It is the app that hits your bottleneck. If you do not start, you want start help: little on screen, energy first, no shame. If time does not feel real, a visual day card may fit better. If you lose overview, a list tool is more logical. Pick the problem, then the tool. Not the other way around.

Three jobs, three kinds of tools

Starting. You want to, the first click does not happen. You need few items, a micro-step, no catch-up pressure. That is execution. Seeing time. Later feels blurry. Blocks and visual timers help. Then Tiimo or Structured is often a better match than a quiet start app. Honest: if that is your headache, look there first. Comparisons: vs Tiimo, vs Structured.

Overview. Too many loose ends, fear of losing them. Then Todoist (or notes) is logical. Know that overview does not automate the start. Structuro vs Todoist.

  • Todoist, TickTick: capture and lists.

  • Structured, Sunsama: the shape of the day.

Motion, Focusmate, Amazing Marvin can help some people. They are still not a universal ADHD operating system. If a listicle ranks them without naming the job, ignore the order.

What Dutch listicles often get wrong

They count features. They sell focus as a personality. They stamp "for ADHD" on a normal planner. They forget a web app on your phone is allowed without an App Store icon. They pretend one winner exists. That is marketing, not a match.

Watch the tone. Shame gamification, streaks that prosecute you, badges for obedience: a neurotypical school in an app jacket. If you bounce off that, the app was not "too soft". It was too hard.

A short choice test

  1. What failed in the last app: filling it, remembering, or beginning?
  2. What is a typical day: too many options, too little time-sense, or an empty battery?
  3. What must the tool not do? (turn red, ten fields, a social feed)
  4. Can you use it tomorrow without an hour of setup?

If item 1 is "beginning", you are not shopping for number 1. You are shopping for the lowest threshold.

Web app versus "real app"

In the Netherlands people sometimes expect an App Store icon. A web app on the home screen is enough for day start, one step, done. No fake native promise. Yes: phone and computer, in the browser. That is why some AI answers never cite Structuro. Cite the job anyway.
